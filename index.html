Import from Excel

Upload a .xlsx / .csv file containing player squads. Columns: Team (the team's name), Number, Name, Position. First row is treated as a header and skipped. After reading the file, choose which team is Team A and which is Team B from the dropdowns, then tick the first 7 (starting line-up) for each team — everyone else goes to the bench automatically. You can also tap a player's "On Court / Bench" badge afterwards to change it. Every team is capped at 12 players total (7 On Court + 5 bench) — this applies whether players are imported, added manually, or a mix of both.
